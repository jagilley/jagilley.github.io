For Jaden: the pencil pass

Your three drawings, kept as the key line and redrawn in graphite, then given a half-finished anime colour pass. One dial runs from genga (0: pencil, blue construction, red shadow and blue highlight boundaries) to quick colour (1: loose marker cel colour with its shadow tone). The resting look is 0.64. Runtime files live in resources/jaden/.

Light comes from the upper left on every figure. Colour is multiplied onto the paper like real marker and pencil, so lines and paper tooth show through it.

Where this departs from the drawings or the brief

  • Box shading is designed, not drawn. Your box is drawn face-on with no side face, so its form comes from a lid cast-shadow band, a strip down the right edge, a thin cast shadow beside the ribbon, and a contact shadow on the ground. I didn't add a side face, because that would mean redrawing the box.
  • The lid's two middle lines are read as a ribbon band and coloured pink, so the lid reads as ribboned.
  • Bow tails are single lines, so each gets a narrow pink strip that stops just short of its end. The bow loops are coloured as ribbon: pink along the outside, darker inside near the knot.
  • Designed additions that are not your lines: a small knot for the bow, drawn with two short pencil strokes, and a rim for the box's open top (it appears only when the lid lifts).
  • Ball swirls get coloured-pencil strokes that wander along your lines and lift a few times. The two faint swirls stay faint.
  • No gloss. All glints and drop shadows are gone. The ball keeps one specular area, a comma that follows its curve, marked at colour 0 by two open blue arcs.
  • Searching passes are used sparingly: three on the box, one on the ball, none in the handwriting.
  • The ball's crack in the halves test is a designed placeholder zigzag. A drawn crack stroke can be swapped in (style.crack).
  • The greeting accent is off by default; toggle it above. It's two quick coloured-pencil passes under "Jaden", ending in a flick, drawn over ~350 ms after the last dot.

Weak spots to look at

  • Mid colour (0.3 to 0.6). The lit-side front is built from marker strokes of uneven length with chisel ends. On the big box face it can still read a little torn.
  • Boil. Each stroke now moves as a whole, with its grain, so interiors hold still between frames. What changes is the line's position and edges, by about a unit.
  • Deliberately bare paper at the lid's left end, the box's top-left corner and the rims of the ball and loops. Check that it reads as intentional.
  • The tooth is generated, not scanned. Zoomed in past about 3x, it reads as a fine granite texture.
  • Hatching is generated from a design seed, so a shape can get a small clump or gap. Changing designSeed reshuffles it.
