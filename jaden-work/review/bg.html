The paper world

The background elements on one sheet, in world units: the figures stand on y = 0 at x = 0, and the sheet spans x −2600…2600, y −2000…700. Everything is drawn by Pencil.render from resources/jaden/bg.style.js. The box or ball is placed on the ground for scale. Notes for the director: style/bg-NOTES.md. Static exports: png/bg-golden.png, bg-dusk.png, bg-golden-color0.png, bg-framings.png, bg-ultrawide.png, bg-hand.png, bg-sketchin.png, bg-dusk-beats.png.

Framings

The same world through three camera boxes: phone portrait, laptop, ultrawide. The sun and the big cloud move in for the portrait layout (layout.tall).
